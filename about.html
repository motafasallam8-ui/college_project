Who We Are

A Family Affair,
A Community Icon

Founded in 2005 by Chef Marco & Sofia Royale, our restaurant began as a small corner bistro with a simple mission: serve honest, delicious food made with love. Today, we are proud to be one of the city's most beloved dining destinations — serving over 2,000 happy guests every month across both our dine-in and delivery services.

2005
Bistro Royale opens its doors with just 12 tables and 4 staff.
2010
Awarded Best New Restaurant by City Dining Guide.
2015
Expanded to a second floor and launched online ordering.
2020
Launched delivery service during pandemic — kept the community fed.
2023
Named Top 10 Restaurants in the Country — Michelin Bib Gourmand.
2025
Celebrating 20 years with a brand-new chef's tasting menu.
